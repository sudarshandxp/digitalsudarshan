About Me

Six years of turning
strategy into pipeline.

I got into digital marketing the way most good marketers do — by being handed a website with almost no traffic and being told to fix it. Six years later, that instinct is still the job: find where a funnel is leaking, and build the system that plugs it.

Today I lead end-to-end digital marketing across SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, website revamps, landing pages, content strategy, analytics and marketing automation. I've planned full website rebuilds from scratch, coordinated directly with developers on structure and speed, and managed paid budgets north of ₹10L a month — while keeping cost-per-lead moving in the right direction.

What sets the work apart isn't any single channel — it's connecting them. SEO feeds content, content feeds paid creative, paid data feeds landing-page decisions, and analytics closes the loop back to strategy. I've run that loop for IT, B2B, travel, hospitality, enterprise and manufacturing brands, adapting the playbook to how each industry actually buys.
